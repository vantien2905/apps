Arrowtwist: Logic Puzzle — Support

Mũi Tên Thoát is a logic puzzle game that works completely offline, with no ads and no account.

How do I play?

Tap an arrow to slide it off the board. It can only leave when the straight line in front of its head is empty.

I lost my progress after reinstalling.

Progress is stored only on your device. Use Settings → Export progress to save a backup file, and Import progress to restore it.

Is there a level that cannot be solved?

No. Every level is generated so that all arrows can leave in some order.
