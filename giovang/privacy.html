Giờ Vàng: Focus Timer — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026

Giờ Vàng is a focus timer that works completely offline. Your privacy is simple: we do not collect any data.

Data you enter

Tasks, tags, focus sessions and settings are stored only on your device, inside the app's private storage. They are never sent to us or to any third party. If device backup (iCloud or computer) is enabled, the data is included in that backup under your own Apple account, which we cannot access.

Notifications and sound

If you allow notifications, Giờ Vàng schedules local notifications on your device when a session ends. Ambient sounds are generated on the device; the microphone is never used.

No accounts, no tracking

Giờ Vàng has no sign-in, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs. It does not use the network and does not track you.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Delete all data in the app, or delete the app, to remove all data permanently.

Children

Giờ Vàng does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
