Tiệm Gọn: Sales Book — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026

Tiệm Gọn is a sales and stock book that works completely offline. Your privacy is simple: we do not collect any data.

Data you enter

Products, orders, customers, credit records and shop details are stored only on your device, inside the app's private storage. They are never sent to us or to any third party. If device backup (iCloud or computer) is enabled, the data is included in that backup under your own Apple account, which we cannot access.

Camera

The camera is used only to read barcodes while the scanner is open. No photo or video is recorded or stored.

No accounts, no tracking, no payments

Tiệm Gọn has no sign-in, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs. It does not use the network, does not track you and does not process payments.

Export and backup

Backups, CSV files and receipts are created on your device and shared only where you choose to send them.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Delete all data in the app, or delete the app, to remove all data permanently.

Children

Tiệm Gọn does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
