Waterly: Drink Water Reminder — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 26, 2026

Giọt Nước (Waterly: Drink Water Reminder) is a water intake tracker that works completely offline. Your privacy is simple: we do not collect any data.

Data you enter

Drinks you log, your daily goal, cups, reminder times and settings are stored only on your device, inside the app's private storage. They are never sent to us or to any third party. If device backup (iCloud or computer) is enabled, the data is included in that backup under your own Apple account, which we cannot access.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules local reminders on your device between the wake and bed times you choose. The "Drink" button on a reminder logs a drink in the app on your device. No health data is read from or written to Apple Health.

Not medical advice

The suggested daily goal is general guidance only. Ask a doctor if you have a condition that limits fluids.

No accounts, no tracking

The app has no sign-in, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs. It does not use the network and does not track you.

Deleting your data

Use the delete or reset option in the app's Settings, or delete the app, to remove all data permanently.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
