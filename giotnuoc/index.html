Dropday: Drink Water Reminder — Support

Giọt Nước is a water intake tracker that works completely offline, with no ads and no account.

Reminders do not appear.

Check iPhone Settings → Notifications → Dropday, and the Reminders tab in the app. Reminders stop for the day once you reach your goal if that option is on.

How do I move my data to a new phone?

Settings → Back up to a file, then Restore from a file on the new phone.

How is the goal calculated?

About 33 ml per kg of body weight, plus extra for exercise or hot weather, kept between 1 and 5 litres.
