Pour & Sort: Color Puzzle — Support

Xếp Màu Nước is a colour-sorting puzzle game that works completely offline, with no ads and no account.

How do I play?

Tap a tube to pick it up, then tap another tube to pour. You can pour onto the same colour or into an empty tube.

I am stuck.

Use Undo, Hint (finds the next move from your current position) or Add tube (once per level).

Some colours look alike.

Turn on Settings → Colour symbols to show a shape on each colour.
