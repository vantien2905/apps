QuietPlay – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

QuietPlay ("the app") is developed by Tien Dinh. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

Summary

  • No account, no sign-up, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • The developer collects no data and runs no servers.
  • Your on/off history and settings are stored only on your device.

How the filter works

When you turn the shield on, the app installs an encrypted DNS (DNS-over-HTTPS) configuration using Apple's NetworkExtension DNS Settings feature. It is not a VPN: your traffic is not routed through any server. Only DNS lookups (the names of the servers your apps connect to) are sent, encrypted, to a free public filtering DNS resolver chosen by your blocking level:

  • Standard: AdGuard DNS public resolver (dns.adguard-dns.com)
  • Family: AdGuard DNS Family resolver (family.adguard-dns.com)

These resolvers are operated by AdGuard, not by the developer, under their own privacy policy: adguard-dns.io/en/privacy.html. You can remove the configuration at any time in the app (Settings > Remove DNS configuration) or in iOS Settings > General > VPN & Device Management > DNS.

The "Check" feature

The check looks up a few well-known ad hostnames and one regular website on your device to see whether they are blocked. The results are shown to you and are not sent anywhere.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page.
