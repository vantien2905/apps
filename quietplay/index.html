QuietPlay: Ad & Tracker Block

Block ads and trackers in games and apps with one tap. No VPN, no account, no data collected.

Set up (once)

  1. Open QuietPlay and tap the shield. When iOS asks, tap Allow.
  2. Open Settings › General › VPN & Device Management › DNS and choose QuietPlay.
  3. Come back to the app: the shield turns green. From now on, turn it on and off right in the app.

FAQ

A game's "watch ad for reward" button doesn't work. That's expected while blocking is on. Turn the shield off, claim the reward, then turn it back on. Tip: touch and hold the app icon for a quick toggle.

I still see some ads. Ads served from a game's own servers can't be blocked by DNS. Use the Check tab to confirm blocking is active; after switching, changes can take about a minute.

I use a VPN or another DNS app. iOS uses one configuration at a time. Pick QuietPlay in the DNS settings.

How do I remove it? In the app: Settings › Remove DNS configuration, or delete the app.
