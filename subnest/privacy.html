Subnest: Subscription Tracker — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 22, 2026

Subnest is designed to work completely offline. Your privacy is simple: we do not collect any data.

Data you enter

Subscriptions, prices, dates, notes and settings you enter are stored only on your device, inside the app's private storage. They are never sent to us or to any third party. If your device backup (iCloud or computer backup) is enabled, the data is included in that backup under your own Apple account, which we cannot access.

No accounts, no tracking

Subnest has no sign-in, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs. The app does not use the network and does not track you across apps or websites.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, Subnest schedules local notifications on your device. They are created and delivered by iOS on your device only.

Export and backup

You can export your data as a CSV or backup file. The file is created on your device and shared only where you choose to send it.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Delete All Data in the app, or delete the app, to remove all data permanently.

Children

Subnest does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
