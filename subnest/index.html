FAQ

Where is my data stored?
Only on your device. Subnest has no servers and no sign-in.

How do I move my data to a new iPhone?
Settings → Export Backup, save the file to Files or AirDrop it, then on the new device use Settings → Restore Backup. Your data is also included in your normal iPhone backup.

Reminders don't arrive.
Open Settings → Reminders in Subnest and make sure they are on, then check iOS Settings → Notifications → Subnest.

Can I use my own currency?
Yes. Settings → Currency lists every ISO currency.
