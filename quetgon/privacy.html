Quét Gọn: Scan PDF & OCR — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026 (updated for the QR & barcode scanner)

Quét Gọn is a document scanner that works offline. Your privacy is simple: we do not collect any data.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only while you scan. Photos you import are chosen by you in the system photo picker. Scanned pages, recognized text and signatures are stored only on your device, inside the app's private storage, and are never uploaded.

Text recognition

Text recognition (OCR) runs on your device using Apple's Vision framework. No image or text is sent to us or to any third party.

QR codes and barcodes

Codes are read on your device and your scan history is stored only on your device. When you open a product barcode, only the barcode number is sent to the free public Open Food Facts database to show the product name; no other information is sent. "Compare prices" and "Search the web" open a web search in Safari, under that website's own privacy policy. Links found in QR codes are opened only when you tap them.

No accounts, no tracking

Quét Gọn has no sign-in, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs. Apart from the product lookups and web searches you start yourself, the app does not use the network, and it does not track you across apps or websites.

Face ID / passcode lock

If you turn on the app lock, iOS verifies your Face ID, Touch ID or passcode. The app never receives or stores biometric data.

Exporting

PDF, JPG and text files are created on your device and shared only where you choose to send them. PDF passwords are not stored.

Deleting your data

Delete documents in the app, use Settings → Delete all data, or delete the app to remove everything permanently. If device backup (iCloud or computer) is enabled, app data is included in that backup under your own Apple account, which we cannot access.

Children

Quét Gọn does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
